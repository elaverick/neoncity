NeonCity: surfaces still textured procedurally
================================================

Everything below is drawn in shaders (noise, hashes and patterns), not from
authored image maps. Line references are to shaders/city.frag unless noted.

Authored (image) materials today, from assets/textures/ (src/render/textures.c):
  - paving_stones_111  sidewalk paving
  - road_012a          road asphalt
  - ground_032         kerb stones, kerb faces, tactile slabs
  - tactile_stud       tactile studs
  - marble_016         lobby: dark marble floors, chequer dark slabs, walls, core cladding, reception desk
  - marble_012         lobby: chequer white slabs, pale core cladding, emblem panels
  - tiles_139          lobby: limestone floor
  - wood_051           lobby: dark wood furniture
  - wood_066           lobby: walnut slat walls
  - wood_095           lobby: light wood furniture and counters
If an authored material's files are missing, its surfaces fall back to the
procedural versions, which still exist in the shader.


STREETS AND GROUND (shadeGround)
--------------------------------
  - Asphalt grime, patched/resurfaced rectangles, wheel ruts and gutter grime (over the road texture)
  - Road paint: lane lines, crossings, glass-bead speckle, wear
  - Manhole covers
  - Kerb-stone / paving joint: grit, dead leaves, moss, grime spill
  - Stains over paving and kerb stones
  - Fine debris (gum, grit, leaves) on sidewalks and lots (debris)
  - Park grass with worn patches
  - Bare lots / waste ground
  - Puddles, wet film, standing water and rain ripples (hollow, ripples)

BUILDING EXTERIORS (shadeFacade, shadeRoof, shadeMetal, shadeDoor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Facade wall palettes: concrete, grimy brick-brown, teal metal panels, pale tile,
    black glass, warm stone, bronze, blue glass
  - Slab edges and pilasters, rust drips under windows, spandrel bands, plant-floor louvres
  - Curtain-wall glass and window glass (reflection, pane sheen, privacy film)
  - Window interiors (interior mapping): room walls, floors, ceilings, furniture
    silhouettes, curtains, blinds, TVs, lights (makeRoom, interiorColor, cornerInterior)
  - Shopfront interiors: shelves of goods (shelves), shop lighting, rolled-down shutters
  - Lobby facade: mullion accent lines
  - Roofs: flat roof membrane, clay tiles on hip roofs, soffits
  - Rooftop metal: plant/vents, concrete, generic metal, solar panels, glass pyramids
  - Doors: glass lobby doors, steel service doors, roller-shutter loading bays with hazard stripes

SIGNS AND LIGHTS (shadeNeon, shadeScreen, shadeEmissive, LED strips)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Neon signs: pseudo-kanji glyphs, tubes, painted boards, metal lip
  - Video screens / billboards: portrait adverts, scrolling tickers, pulsing rings, brand marks, LED grid
  - Street lamp heads, aircraft warning lights, emissive boxes
  - LED strips (steady and chasing)

ARCOLOGIES (shadeArcology)
--------------------------
  - Street, floor and slat paving (wet concrete pavers, terrazzo, tile, rubber, etc.)
  - Coffered ceilings with bare tubes
  - Megastructure concrete: columns, capitals, beams, portals (board marks, grime, wet streaks),
    fluted stone piers, posters on columns
  - Block masses: painted concrete, corrugated sheet, small windows, capsule portholes, graffiti
  - Roofs and puddles, stall interiors (goods, menu boards), stall canopies
  - Shutters, counters, the express spine, cores (hazard skirts)
  - Galleries and bridges, soffits with lamps, fascias, unit fronts (unitFronts)
  - Rails and glass balustrades, metalwork, the glazed crown, sky lobby glazing
  - Crates, AC units, awnings, terrace planters, stairs, linings, kiosks, lift doors
  - Steam and searchlight shafts
